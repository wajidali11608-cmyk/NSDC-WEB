# NSDC-JHSC — Digital Tech Club

## Mission
We are a student-led collective building at the intersection of design, code, and the future. Based at Jamia Hamdard University, New Delhi, we operate across nine specialized divisions with a focus on high-fidelity digital experiences and technical excellence.

## Core Identity
- **Name**: NSDC-JHSC (Student Collective)
- **Philosophy**: Build your highest vision. The premier student collective architecting the future in silence.
- **Location**: New Delhi, IN

## Divisions
The collective is organized into nine specialized divisions:
- **01 Core**: Architects of the vision, managing strategic directives and operational logic.
- **02 Tech**: The vanguard of engineering, building Nexus infrastructure and high-performance systems.
- **03 Data Science**: The intelligence layer, focused on neural networks and generative systems.
- **04 Media**: Visual chroniclers, producing cinema-grade media and cinematic direction.
- **05 Content**: Keepers of the thesis, crafting written narratives and technical editorial.
- **06 Social Media**: The pulse of the collective, orchestrating digital presence and engagement.
- **07 Management**: The structural spine, maintaining organizational health and logistics.
- **08 Creative**: The aesthetic vanguard, exploring interface theory and dynamic identity.
- **09 Partnerships**: The external interface, building industrial bridges and industry pipelines.

## Technical Stack
- **Framework**: [TanStack Start](https://tanstack.com/router/latest/docs/framework/react/start/overview) (React + TanStack Router + Vite)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Components**: [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com/) (Primitive-based high-fidelity components)
- **State Management**: [TanStack Query](https://tanstack.com/query/latest)
- **Runtime/Build**: Bun (Recommended) / Vite
- **Animations**: Custom kinetic motions, Aurora glass effects, and marquee systems.

## Key Features
- **Luminous Prism Design**: A world-class, "Awwwards-level" interface featuring glassmorphism and liquid typography.
- **Dynamic Clock & Scroll**: Integrated real-time IST clock and visual scroll progress indicators.
- **Responsive Layout**: Fluid corner-anchored architecture that adapts from desktop monoliths to mobile interfaces.
- **Project Tracking**: Automated "field notes" and "recent operations" log systems.
